VER. 3.5 — DESKTOP MAINTENANCE
Windows, Linux and macOS; the Android APK is unchanged.
The physical south button runs in the world and confirms native menus/text.
West interacts once per press. Kit 3 is Ultimate, with endgame equipment.
Post-victory acceleration defaults on; 15% recovery defaults off. Free map
without the item is disabled. God Menu unlock lasts until process exit;
heal/revive restores the party and its overworld appearance.
Boss-phase sprite references, per-layer horizontal/vertical background
raster effects, PSI routing, black intertitles, camera shake restart and
mouse battle-target selection have been corrected.

EXPERIMENTAL CO-OP
P2 starts with P1 equipment, two Bread rolls and one Cookie. Only the
Bubble Monkey may accompany both players. Both players can trigger story
dialogue/actions and command Paula/Jeff/Poo during joint battles.
Distant exploration uses a vertical P1/P2 split. Joining combat requires
physical contact; Waiting for next turn appears while the join is pending.
A flash marks joining combat. P2 L3 teleports to P1; R3 cycles six saved
clothing palettes. P2 Select leaves with a photo pose; a joint battle must
finish first to preserve turn and party state. Stick clicks inside F1/F2
still trigger Mr. Saturn. Mess windows use a blue tint.
Rolling HP, protagonist identity, party rebuilding, sprite occlusion,
independent doors/hotspots, shared music and contact snapshots were fixed.

Windows validation uses simulated SDL controllers and native integration
tests. Linux/macOS binaries are compiled/inspected, not executed here.
The full campaign and every physical controller model are not validated.
Co-op remains experimental. Keep backup copies of personal saves.

DESKTOP CONTROLLER UPDATE
Physical south: Run in the world / OK in native menus (PS X / Xbox A / Switch B / generic 1).
Physical west: interact and confirm in-game (Square / X / Y / 3).
Physical east: personal menu and back (Circle / B / A / 2).
Physical north: party HP and money (Triangle / Y / X / 4).
Disable Run in F1 to use the south button for interaction/confirmation.
F1/F2 always use south for OK and east for Back, including custom bindings.
Click either stick (L3/R3) in F1 to interact with Mr. Saturn.
Old A/B/C presets migrate; player-customized bindings are preserved.
The rose has solid collision and sits on the ground right of the tree behind
Ness's house. It no longer draws over characters or the opening cinematics.
Its final line is "Funny, I don't remember who planted this here."
The "Later that night..." prologue card is translated in Portuguese.
Existing Android APK files are unchanged by this desktop update.

GETTING STARTED
The original local installation opens with EarthBound PC.exe on Windows.
Keep SDL2.dll beside the executable. It opens without a command prompt.

The separate "Earthbound PC Port" folder contains Windows, Linux and macOS
versions which require your own game ROM at every launch. Place your own
unmodified EarthBound (USA) ROM in "Coloque sua Room aqui" beside the system
folders. Accepted files: .sfc or .smc, 3 MiB, with or without a 512-byte copier
header. Extract ZIP files first. Do not use an already translated ROM.
No ROM is included or downloaded. The ROM is read without being modified.
adaptacao.ebpatch reconstructs the required game resources in memory.
Keep that file with each system's version.

Windows 10/11 x64: open EarthBound PC.exe in Port Windows (or Port PC).
Linux x64, glibc 2.36+: run ./earthbound-pc in Port Linux; keep lib beside it.
If necessary, mark the program executable in its file properties.
macOS 11+, Intel/Apple Silicon: open EarthBound PC.app in Port Mac.
This local application is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks it, approve
it through System Settings > Privacy & Security after checking its source.
The shared ROM folder may instead be placed inside one system's folder
when copying that version to another computer.

YOUR SAVES
Each separate port stores its settings and saves in its own Saves folder.
It does not automatically import or overwrite the original installation.
To continue an older game, COPY its .srm, drawer-*.ebs and slot-*.ebs files
into the appropriate Saves folder. Keep the original files as a backup.
Older V2 states are supported. New V3 states require version 2.5 or later.
The original installation keeps using its previous preferences directory.

MENUS AND BASIC CONTROLS
F1: video, audio, controls, saves, chapters and optional gameplay features.
F2: open Saves directly. Three drawers A/B/C, with 12 slots each.
F3: choose Quicksave 1, 2, 3 or 4 to SAVE (slots 9, 10, 11 and 12).
F4: choose one of those quicksaves to LOAD. Escape/Q/Ctrl cancels.
The four quicksave slots are yellow. Saving plays the original photo pose
and shutter; loading gives a short photo flash without moving the party.
"Continue from Save State" is selected first when a valid state exists.
The small EarthBound-style "Menu F1" box is clickable and opens the options.
The main menu also accepts Enter to confirm and Backspace to go back.

WASD/arrows: move or navigate. E: interact/confirm. Q/Esc/Ctrl: back in menus.
Tab: Ness's menu. Shift + movement: optional Run, 23% faster.
Comma/period: zoom out/in, from 50% to 150%. Indicator hides after 2 seconds.
Hold +: 4x fast-forward with normal audio speed/pitch. Hold -: rewind.
M: the game's map shortcut. With "Map without the item (M)" enabled in
F1 > Gameplay, the map works without carrying the Town Map item.
Areas without a native town map remain unavailable. This option defaults off.
F5–F12 have no assigned shortcuts.

MOUSE
Hold left click in a direction to walk; click a character/object to approach
and interact. Left click selects/confirms. Right click opens Ness's menu or
goes back. Blank left clicks are ignored. Holding a menu selection does not
accelerate the dialogue it opens; release and click again to advance text.
The wheel moves through menu choices, party members and enemy targets,
following the layout/context. It also adjusts digits when entering numbers.
Mouse movement can be turned off in F1 > Gameplay.

CONTROLLERS
PS4, PS5, Xbox, Nintendo Switch and SDL-compatible generic controllers are
supported. Choose presets A (Original), B (Modern) or C (Simple), or customize
your bindings under F1 > Controls. The illustrated map follows your bindings.
The external Controles folder also includes reference diagrams.
F1/F2 menus retain standard navigation when gameplay buttons are customized.
Back + Start opens F1 as an alternative to a reserved system/Home button.
Gameplay hints show the currently assigned Run button.

VIDEO, LANGUAGES AND OPTIONAL FEATURES
1920x1080 widescreen is supported, with extra scenery at the sides.
Choose window/fullscreen, 30/60 presentation FPS, colors, scaling and CRT
filters in F1. Game logic remains at 60 Hz; audio has its own normal clock.
Choose English or Brazilian Portuguese for the game, and independently
choose the language of the F1/F2 menus. The supplied fan translation retains
distinctive game elements such as SMAAAASH. No full human review was done.
Text speed, Run, Where to go?, post-victory acceleration, Run visuals,
mouse control, map access and recovery after defeat can be configured.
Optional defeat recovery respawns the party nearby with 15% HP and temporary
invincibility, preserving progress and spent items.

CHAPTERS AND KITS
Choose a location, then a kit, then confirm the chapter start.
This replaces the current unsaved session; saved files are kept.
Balanced keeps the checkpoint's equipment. Exploration uses a modestly
lighter weapon/armor selection with extra money. Combat favors stronger
available protection and appropriate Jeff rockets, with less money.
Armor alternatives are restricted to equipment present in checkpoints up
to that chapter, with bounded defense changes. Essential items and Poo's
special equipment are preserved. The selection does not unlock later areas.

VERSION 3.0 CHANGES
- EarthBound Ver.3.0 header and a clickable Credits button in F1.
- An inspectable rose behind Ness's house, dedicated to Amanda.
- Clickable Menu F1 reminder; stable native mouse menus and release guards.
- Click the volume setting to change it by 10%.
- God Menu under Gameplay: double-click its padlock, read the warning and
  choose Yes. Infinite party HP/PP, healing/removing ailments, character
  weapons and armor, Jeff tools, food, all items, noclip and +5,000 money.
  Items require a free inventory space. Cheats can break progression; keep
  a separate save. All cheats start disabled when reopening the application.
- Previous Summers stairs, chapter distortion, armor and save fixes retained.


Version 2.5 fixes are retained: battle-state cold loads, consistent camera
shutter, 36 slots, four quicksaves, 400x225 thumbnails and improved old previews.
