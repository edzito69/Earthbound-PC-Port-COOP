INSTALLATION

Place your own original, unmodified EarthBound (USA) ROM, in .sfc or .smc format, inside the "Coloque sua Room aqui" folder, located alongside the operating system folders.

Accepted ROM size: 3 MiB, optionally including a 512-byte copier header.

No ROM is provided, downloaded, or modified by this project.

Keep the adaptacao.ebpatch file.

Windows: Port Windows/EarthBound PC COOP 3.5.exe

Linux: Port Linux/earthbound-pc, with the lib folder next to the executable (x64, glibc 2.36 or newer).

Mac: Port Mac/EarthBound PC COOP.app, compatible with macOS 11 or newer, supporting both Intel and Apple Silicon processors.

The Mac application is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, allow the application through the system's Privacy & Security settings.

HOW TO ENABLE CO-OP MODE

Load your game, open F1 > Gameplay > Experimental co-op, accept the warning, and apply the settings.

CO-OP MODE IS DISABLED BY DEFAULT.

Press Start/Options/+/Menu on the controller intended for Player 2 (P2), then confirm joining the game.

Selecting "No" keeps the controller available for Player 1 (P1).

P1 can use a keyboard and mouse while P2 uses a controller. Controllers not assigned to P2 can also operate P1.

Meet Mess!

Mess is a variation of Ness featuring blue stripes, a purple cap, and a special photo pose when spawning into the game!

He also has independent character attributes, including:

His own character statistics;

Individual HP and PP;

Personal inventory and equipment;

Experience and progression based on P1's progress.

Cash rewards earned during shared battles are deposited into P1's party account.

Mess's personal wallet is preserved when entering and leaving shared battles.

The main story party follows P1.

Both players can access their own menus and conversations while the other player continues moving freely.

When players move far apart, the screen splits vertically into two independent panels, one for P1 and another for P2.

Scene transitions carry both players together. Any ongoing battle must be completed before leaving the area.

Both players can trigger essential story dialogues and events.

BATTLES AND SAVING

During individual battles, combat occupies half of the screen while the other player remains free to explore the world.

To join an ongoing battle, simply approach the player who is fighting. The battle then expands to fill the entire screen.

P1 controls Ness, while P2 controls Mess. Both players can also issue commands to other party members.

The system supports all four original heroes plus Mess, allowing a total of five playable characters.

Save states from F1/F2, quicksaves from F3/F4, and the rewind feature restore both players simultaneously.

V4 co-op save states require this experimental build.

Save files are kept separate from those used by the standard port.

In-game phone saves preserve P1's campaign progress. To preserve Mess as well, use F1/F2 save states.

The game uses a single shared audio clock, playing one priority music track at a time.

Always back up your save files!

Co-op mode cannot be disabled during a shared battle.

If a controller disconnects, reconnect it and press Start to resume controlling Mess. Alternatively, disable co-op outside a shared battle.

The PlayStation Cross (X) button allows players to run in the overworld and confirm native menus and text.

Running increases movement speed by 23%.

Disabling the Run option also allows the bottom face button to be used for interaction and confirmation.

In the F1/F2 menus, the bottom face button always confirms, while the right face button always goes back, regardless of custom bindings.

Pressing either analog stick (L3 or R3) while inside the F1 menu activates Mr. Saturn.

Shoulder buttons switch between menu tabs.

Additional Controller Shortcuts

F1 Menu: PS/Home/Xbox button.

F2 Menu: Share/Create/View/Capture button.

Zoom: L3 + triggers.

Reset Zoom: R3.

Rewind: Left trigger.

Fast-Forward: Right trigger.

For generic controllers:

Back + shoulder buttons: Rewind and fast-forward.

L3 + shoulder buttons: Zoom.

Back + Start: Opens the F1 menu.

TESTING AND EXPERIMENTAL LIMITATIONS

Windows testing includes:

Two simulated SDL controllers;

Independent player menus;

Both players' combat turns;

Five characters participating in battle;

Scene transitions and player following;

Rewind functionality;

Photography events;

Save states loaded from a fresh launch, including overworld exploration, individual battles, and shared battles.

Linux and macOS versions have been compiled and inspected but have not been executed on their respective operating systems.

The entire campaign and all physical controller models have not yet been tested.

With a full main party, the battle system has room for only one additional NPC companion.

A second companion remains preserved in P1's party outside combat.
