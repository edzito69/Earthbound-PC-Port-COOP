EARTHBOUND Ver.3.5 COOP — EXPERIMENTAL
Version by Edzito, with much love.

Place your own unmodified EarthBound (USA) .sfc/.smc ROM in "Coloque sua Room
aqui", beside the system folders. Accepted: 3 MiB, optionally with a 512-byte
copier header. No ROM is supplied, downloaded or modified. Keep adaptacao.ebpatch.
Windows: Port Windows/EarthBound PC COOP 3.5.exe. Linux: Port Linux/earthbound-pc,
with lib beside it (x64, glibc 2.36+). Mac: Port Mac/EarthBound PC COOP.app,
macOS 11+ for Intel and Apple Silicon. The local Mac application is not notarized;
if blocked, allow it through the system's Privacy & Security settings.

JOINING
Load a game, open F1 > Gameplay > Experimental co-op, accept the warning and
apply. It starts disabled. Press Start/Options/+/Menu on the controller intended
for P2 and confirm. No keeps it available to P1. P1 can use keyboard/mouse and
P2 a single controller; unassigned controllers can also operate P1.
Mess has blue stripes, a purple cap, a photo pose on arrival and independent
stats, HP/PP, inventory, equipment and experience, based on P1's progression.
Joint battle cash rewards go to P1's party account; Mess's personal wallet
remains preserved when joining and leaving shared battles.
The story party follows P1. Both players can use personal menus/dialogues while
the other keeps moving. Distant players get separate vertical P1/P2 panels.
Scene transitions carry both players; finish an ongoing battle before leaving.
Both players can trigger essential story dialogue and events.

BATTLES / SAVES
Solo combat uses half the screen while the other player remains in the world.
Approach the battling player to join; combat expands to the full screen. P1
controls Ness, P2 controls Mess; both may command companions. Four original heroes plus Mess are supported.
F1/F2 states, F3/F4 quicksaves and rewind restore both players together. V4 co-op
states require this experimental build. Saves are separate from the normal port.
Phone saves keep P1's campaign; use F1/F2 states to preserve Mess as well.
One shared audio clock plays one priority music track at a time. Back up saves.
Co-op cannot be disabled during a joint battle. After a disconnect, reconnect
and press Start to resume Mess, or disable co-op outside a joint battle.

DEFAULT CONTROLS
PS: X runs in the world and confirms native menus/text; Square interacts/confirms; Circle personal menu/back;
Triangle HP/money. Xbox: A/X/B/Y. Switch: B/Y/A/X. Generic: 1/3/2/4.
Running adds 23%. Disable Run to also use south for interaction/confirmation.
F1/F2 always use south for OK and east for Back, regardless of custom bindings.
Either stick click in F1 activates Mr. Saturn. Shoulders switch tabs.
F1: PS/Home/Xbox; F2: Share/Create/View/Capture. L3+triggers zoom; R3 resets;
left trigger rewinds, right trigger fast-forwards. Generic controllers use
Back+shoulders for rewind/turbo and L3+shoulders for zoom. Back+Start opens F1.
Keyboard: WASD/arrows, E/Enter OK, Q/Esc/Ctrl Back, Tab personal menu, Shift Run,
M optional map, comma/period zoom, +/- turbo/rewind. F3/F4 choose quicksave slots.
Presets/customization and illustrated guides are available in F1 and Controles.

This release includes the new controller layout, solid rose at Edzito's selected
ground position right of the tree behind Ness's house, corrected dedication and
Portuguese "Later that night..." card. Existing Android APKs are unchanged;
there is no Android co-op build.

TESTING / EXPERIMENTAL LIMITS
Windows tests cover two simulated SDL controllers, independent menus, both
players' turns, five heroes, scene following, rewind, photographs and cold-start
world/solo/shared battle states. Linux/macOS are built and inspected, not run.
The entire campaign and all physical controller models have not been tested.
With a full party, combat has room for one additional NPC companion; a second
companion remains preserved in P1's party outside combat.
