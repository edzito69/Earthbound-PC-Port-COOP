EarthBound PC — Ver. 3.6
Platforms: Windows, Linux, and macOS
Release: October 25, 2026
Latest update: October 26, 2026

ADDED
Automatic saves: Dedicated autosave slot in each drawer under F1/F2 → Saves. Saves trigger when changing towns, passing through doors, and winning battles, after transitions finish.
Simultaneous battle commands: P1 and Mess select actions in adjacent native menus. Actions execute once both players finish selecting, preserving the original targeting, item, and turn rules. Save states retain selections in progress.
Mouse-controlled P2: With co-op enabled, double-click the right mouse button to request control of P2. Keyboard and unassigned controllers remain assigned to P1.
Independent exploration: Optional support for players exploring separate rooms.
Photo pose shortcut: Press the bottom face button + R1/RB to pose in the overworld.
Support Monkey: Optional cosmetic companion for P1 under F1 → Gameplay, disabled by default and independent of the story companion.
God Menu teleportation: Destinations include towns, pizzerias, pharmacies, weapon shops, and other establishments. Teleportation preserves inventory and story progress.
Changed
Experimental co-op is now available in every desktop edition. Enable it under F1 → Gameplay; it starts disabled.
English is the default language for both the game and F1/F2 menus. Portuguese remains selectable.
The P2 join prompt appears directly over gameplay without opening F1.
Both players perform the photo pose when P2 joins, accompanied by a cheerful sound effect while preserving the music.
Balanced and Exploration kits provide weaker equipment and leave more inventory space.
Mess receives equipment equivalent to P1’s and a copy of half the eligible food/healing items in P1’s active leader inventory, rounded down and limited by available space. P1 keeps the original items.
Ordinary item pickups are shared between players. Quest items are not duplicated, and internal transfers do not create extra copies. Overflow copies go to Escargo.
Battle reward deposits credit both players with the same amount.
Story progress is shared, allowing either player to continue without repeating completed quest dialogue.
In co-op, a defeated player returns to the overworld with 15% HP while the other can continue fighting. Single-player follows the Gameplay preference.
Gameplay and co-op settings apply immediately without requiring Apply.
Controls
P1: Press T on the keyboard or L3 to teleport to P2.
P2: Press L3 to teleport to P1.
Teleportation waits for a safe moment outside battles and dialogue. L3 + triggers remains reserved for zoom.
P2 R3: Cycles through six clothing palettes. Pajamas follow Ness’s current appearance.
P2 Select: Leaves the session without opening F2 on release.
Triangle: Displays party HP/PP alongside money.
Saves and Distribution
Distribution ZIPs exclude ROMs, personal saves, and preferences.
Updating does not overwrite existing saves.
Use F1/F2 save states to preserve both players.
Original telephone saves continue to store P1’s campaign.
Testing and Known Limitations
Windows builds were executed and tested.
Linux and macOS builds were compiled and inspected, but not executed on those systems.
Co-op remains experimental.
The entire campaign has not been totally validated.
